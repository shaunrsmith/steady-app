A gentle companion for perfectionist spirals

What it is

Steady is a small, kind app for the days when your mind won't stop replaying, checking, comparing, or telling you that nothing you do is enough.

It doesn't gamify your mental health. No streaks, no scores, no guilt. Just simple practices that help you put thoughts down:

  • Check In — notice how the spirals are today, 1 to 5
  • Breathe — guided box breathing
  • Reframe — a gentler angle on the perfectionist thought
  • Wins & Good Enough — log small wins, draw the line for the day
  • Compassion, Compare Detox, Mattering — talk to yourself like someone you love
  • Wind Down & End of Day — put the day's thoughts down before sleep

Private by design

There is no account, no tracking, and no server. Everything you write stays in your browser, on your device. You can download a backup of your data anytime from the Progress tab — and that file goes only where you put it.

Install it like an app

Steady works offline once installed. It takes about ten seconds:

iPhone / iPad

Open this site in Safari → tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen."

Android

Open this site in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app").

Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

A note on what Steady is not

Steady is a self-help companion, not therapy, diagnosis, or medical care. If the spirals feel bigger than an app — and sometimes they are — that's not a failure. It means you deserve more support than an app can give.

In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), anytime. Anywhere else, findahelpline.com can point you to someone local.

Steady over spirals. You're doing enough.
